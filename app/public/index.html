On-device estimate · validated against DXA

Body composition from a phone scan

Tell us your height, then turn slowly in front of the camera for five seconds. Everything runs inside this browser tab — no video, photo or measurement is ever uploaded.

Starting up...

1 · About you

Your height is the ruler. The camera cannot know how big you are, so every measurement is scaled from the height you give here — make it accurate.

2 · Capture

Prop the phone up so your whole body fits. The app walks you through three positions, confirming each before moving on — nothing is rushed.

Wear fitted clothing. The outline is the measurement, so fabric standing off your body is read as body. Measured on this pipeline: a normal t-shirt adds about +5 cm to the waist and +1.6 body-fat points; a loose hoodie adds +9–12 cm. Bare torso or close-fitting clothes give the honest number.

    If your whole body will not fit, switch to a wider lens rather than backing further away. Zoom does not affect accuracy — scale comes from your height, not the camera.

      How much to trust this
      
      
      
      
      Accuracy drops for very lean bodies, where the training data is thinnest,
      and the model tends to read about 1.4 points low for people of Asian descent — a known
      limit of measuring body composition from body shape. Using several frames reduces random
      error only; it does not remove that bias.
